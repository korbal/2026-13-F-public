Vizsgaremek: concept

What to write for next week's lesson. Filled-in example · Back to home

Format: one A4 page, plus one hand-drawn screen sketch (a photo is fine). One per team.

“It'll be good”, “etc.” and “something like that” are not answers. Write concrete names, data and steps everywhere. Look at the example: this is the level of detail.

1Team and title

Names (2–3 people) and a working title for the project.

2The problem (3–5 sentences)

  • Who has this problem? Name a real person or group. E.g. “my uncle's hair salon”, not “the users”.
  • How do they solve it today? A notebook, Excel, a Messenger group…
  • What's wrong with that?

3Roles

Who uses the app, and what can each of them do? E.g. guest, registered user, admin.

4User stories (6–10)

Use this pattern: “As a [role], I want to [what], so that [why].”

Mark each one MUST (no app without it) or NICE TO HAVE (if there is time left).

5One concrete scenario

One made-up user's whole journey, step by step, with real data.

E.g.: “Kiss Anna opens it on her phone, picks Thursday 17:00 with Pethő Gábor, enters her name and gets a confirmation. The salon owner sees the booking in the daily list on the computer.”

6Data

  • What things does the app store? E.g. User, Appointment, Service.
  • For each: 3–5 fields and one filled-in sample row.
  • The relationships in words. E.g. “one user can have many bookings”.

7Screens

  • A list of all screens, one sentence each.
  • One hand drawing of the main screen, on a phone.
  • What does the user do on the phone, and what on the computer?

8Who does what

Everyone has their own part that they present alone at the defence, in Hungarian and in English. E.g. “Kovács: database + backend endpoints; Nagy: the phone interface.”

9What will NOT be in it

At least 3 things you leave out on purpose.

10Questions, risks

What are you unsure about? What can't you build yet?

11English summary

3–5 sentences in English: what the app is for and who uses it. The defence has a 3–5 minute English part too; this is the first step.

Not accepted if:
  • there is no real user,
  • there is no stored data (a game, a calculator or a static page),
  • one team member's part is not visible,
  • the scenario only says general things.
