This is a made-up team's filled-in concept. This is what an acceptable one looks like: concrete people, concrete data, concrete steps. Don't pick this topic; write up your own the same way.

1Team and title

Team: Minta Péter, Példa Zsófia
Working title: Ollóka, a booking app for a small hair salon

2The problem

The Hajvarázs salon in Győr has two hairdressers (Pethő Gábor and Szabó Edit). Today customers book by phone or on Messenger, and the hairdressers write it into a paper notebook. While they work they often can't pick up the phone, so they lose customers. Sometimes two customers get written down for the same time. In the evening nobody sees in one place how much money came in that day.

3Roles

RoleWhat they can do
Guest (not logged in)sees services, prices and free time slots
Registered customerbooks, cancels a booking, sees past bookings
Hairdressersees their own daily list, sets days off
Admin (the owner)manages services and hairdressers, sees daily income

4User stories

#Story
1As a guest, I want to see the free time slots, so that I don't have to call.MUST
2As a registered customer, I want to book a hairdresser for a service, so that my place is certain.MUST
3As a registered customer, I want to cancel my booking, so that someone else can get the slot.MUST
4As a hairdresser, I want to see today's bookings in time order, so that I know who is coming.MUST
5As the admin, I want to add a new service (name, price, length), so that the price list is up to date.MUST
6As a hairdresser, I want to set a day off, so that nobody can book that day.MUST
7As the admin, I want to see daily and monthly income, so that I see how the business is doing.NICE TO HAVE
8As a registered customer, I want an email reminder the day before, so that I don't forget.NICE TO HAVE

5One concrete scenario

  1. Kiss Anna opens Ollóka on her phone at 21:00.
  2. She picks the Női hajvágás (women's haircut) service (6 500 Ft, 45 minutes).
  3. She picks Pethő Gábor, then Thursday. The app only shows the slots where 45 minutes fit: 9:00, 10:00, 14:15, 17:00.
  4. She picks 17:00. She has no account yet, so she registers (name, email, phone number, password).
  5. The confirmation appears: “Thursday 17:00–17:45, Pethő Gábor, Női hajvágás, 6 500 Ft”.
  6. On Thursday morning Pethő Gábor opens his daily list on the salon computer: Kiss Anna is in the 17:00 row with her phone number.
  7. If someone else then wants to book Gábor for Thursday, 17:00 no longer shows up for them.

9What will NOT be in it

  • online payment (they pay in the salon)
  • several salons (there is only one)
  • a native mobile app (it will be a responsive web client)
  • ratings and reviews

10Questions, risks

  • How do we work out the free slots when services have different lengths (30, 45, 90 minutes)?
  • What happens if two customers book the same slot in the same second?
  • We can't build login (password, session) yet.

11English summary

Ollóka is a booking app for a small hair salon. Customers see free time slots on their phone and book an appointment in three steps instead of calling. Hairdressers see their daily list on the salon computer, and the owner manages services and prices. It replaces the paper notebook and prevents double bookings.
